## FAQ Copy

**Q: Is StackSpend really free?**
A: Yes. The audit tool is completely free. We monetize through Credex consulting engagements for teams that want expert help implementing changes.

**Q: How accurate are the recommendations?**
A: All recommendations use deterministic, finance-reasoned rules based on official pricing data. We flag the confidence level for each recommendation.

**Q: Does StackSpend use AI to make decisions?**
A: No. The audit logic is 100% rule-based. AI (Claude 3.5) is only used to write the executive summary — and even that has a fallback.
